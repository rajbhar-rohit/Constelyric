✦ Free & open source · Windows 10/11

Lyrics that bloom into constellations

Constelyric lights up the words of whatever you’re playing, one word at a time, and turns them into a glowing constellation on your screen.

⬇ Download for Windows Portable .zip
Works with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, VLC and more.

Three ways to see your lyrics

Pick a style from the tray panel at any time.

☰

Stack

Three lines glide up as the song moves on, with a karaoke wipe across every word.

◎

Focus

One line at a time. Upcoming words float up into place just before they’re sung.

✦

Constellation

Fullscreen. Every word becomes a star, linked into a web, inside a soft ambient glow.

Install in 30 seconds

DownloadGrab the installer. No Python needed, no admin rights.
Run itFollow the short wizard. Optionally start with Windows.
Play a songClick the star in your system tray to pick a style.
Seeing “Windows protected your PC”? New apps that aren’t code-signed yet trigger this. Click More info, then Run anyway. Constelyric is open source, so you can read every line of the code first.
